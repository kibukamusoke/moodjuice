🧠 App Idea: “MoodJuice – Sip Your Vibe Daily**”

Tagline: An emotional espresso shot for your soul.

⸻

🪄 What it does:
	•	Every day, you get one “emotional juice” flavor based on a combo of:
	•	Your mood (picked via emoji)
	•	Random world events
	•	Your horoscope (because, why not?)

Then, it generates:
	•	A drink name (e.g., Spicy Anxiety Latte, Chill Mango Regret Smoothie)
	•	A vibe breakdown (funny one-liner and animated blob)
	•	A suggested activity (e.g., “Go touch grass.” “Call your mom.” “Do 10 squats and scream.”)
	•	A custom emoji cocktail for your current mood

⸻

🔧 Tech Stack (for funzies & practice):
	•	Frontend: Flutter (you already ride with it)
	•	Backend: Supabase (quick af to set up)
	•	Optional Extras:
	•	AI to generate drink names (like OpenAI or even a local model for fun)
	•	Daily notifications: “Time for your mood juice 🍹”

⸻

🤳 Why it’s not useless:
	•	Helps you check in with yourself without feeling like therapy
	•	It’s memeable
	•	It’s shareable (you know people will post their MoodJuice on IG stories)
	•	It’s great UX practice
	•	Could easily turn into a social feature or even a mental health startup… 👀
